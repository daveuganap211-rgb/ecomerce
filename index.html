📜 Rules & Regulations

Please read these before you log in or create an account.

Accounts

  • Give accurate information when you sign up. One account per person.
  • You are responsible for everything done under your account, so keep your password to yourself.

Orders & delivery

  • Placing an order is a commitment to receive and pay for it. Cash on Delivery orders are paid to the courier.
  • Every order moves through three stages: To Ship, To Deliver and To Receive. Please press “Order Received” once your parcel arrives.
  • Check your parcel when it arrives and report any damage right away.

Vouchers

  • One voucher can be used per order. Minimum spend, category and item-count conditions apply.
  • Vouchers have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and may be voided if misused.

Conduct

  • Be honest and respectful in ratings and feedback. No abusive, false or spam content.
  • Prices, stock and delivery times shown are for this demo shop and may change.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Here is how your information is handled.

What we keep

  • Your name, email, phone number, delivery address, cart, vouchers and order history.
  • Your profile picture, if you add one. It never leaves this device.

Where it is kept

  • This is a demo shop. Your data is stored only in this browser and is not sent to any server or sold or shared with anyone.
  • Because it is a demo, passwords are saved in your browser as typed. Please do not reuse a real password from another site.

Payments

  • No real payment is taken. GCash, bank transfer and cash on delivery are for demonstration only.

Staying safe

  • Log out when you use a shared computer or phone.
  • You can clear your cart or order history any time from Settings in the account menu.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, open the cart, pick a voucher if you have one, then press Proceed to Checkout and fill in your delivery details.

How do vouchers work?

Claim vouchers in the Voucher Center, then apply one in your cart. Each order can use one voucher, and each voucher has its own minimum spend or category.

What do To Ship, To Deliver and To Receive mean?

To Ship: your parcel is being prepared. To Deliver: the courier has it and is on the way. To Receive: it has arrived, so press “Order Received” in My Orders.

When is shipping free?

Orders of ₱5,000 or more ship free. Below that, shipping is ₱150, or free with the FREESHIP voucher.

Which payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery, GCash and Bank Transfer. This is a demo shop, so no real payment is taken.

I forgot something in my profile

Open Profile in this menu, update your details and press Save Changes. Your address is reused at checkout.

Still stuck? Email help@desoundsource.com (demo address).
